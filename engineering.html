Sixth form · Engineering · 2026-27

Engineering beyond the classroom38 things to make, enter, watch and read. Free unless the entry says otherwise.

Engineering admissions, apprenticeship applications and the professional bodies all ask the same question: what have you made? The competitions and placements below give you something to make and a deadline to make it by. The talks and reading are for the interview, where you will be asked why it worked. Each time you do something, write one line in your logbook: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.
