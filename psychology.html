Sixth form · Psychology · 2026-27

Psychology beyond the classroom36 things to listen to, read, watch and enter. All free.

Psychology is a science degree, and admissions tutors want to see that you know it: research methods, statistics and the studies behind the headlines. The podcasts and reading here are chosen for that. Use the essay prizes to practise arguing from evidence. Each time you do something, write one line in your research diary: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Competitions · 2 things

Enter

Np

National Psychology Poster Competition (Royal Holloway, University of London)

29entryY12, in teams of 2–5; up to three teams per schoolvia school

A team competition for Y12. You make a short video and an academic poster answering a set question (2026: "How can psychology help us build a healthier and stronger community?") and present it to judges at Royal Holloway. Edgbaston High School won Best Video in 2026.

When: Registration has opened in January; the 2026 competition day was Monday 15 June at Royal Holloway (Egham). Confirm 2027 dates with the department.

Royal Holloway  royalholloway.ac.uk/studying-here/schools-and-colleges/activities-by-subject/psychology/psychology-resources/national-psychology-competition

Ub

UK Brain Bee (part of the International Brain Bee)

30paperUK secondary pupils aged 13–18 studying GCSE, A level or equivalententer yourself

A neuroscience quiz competition. Online first round in March; top scorers go to a final at Imperial College London. The study guide is free on the site and overlaps with Biopsychology.

When: 2026 pattern: online preliminary round in March, national final at Imperial College London in April, international final in November. Confirm 2027 dates.

UK Brain Bee  ukbrainbee.com/competition-ukbrainbee

34applyY12 at state schools; priority for under-represented backgrounds

A free week in Oxford studying psychology, with support afterwards if you apply. Check the selection criteria.

When: 2026 pattern: applications opened 15 December 2025 and closed midday 13 January 2026. UNIQ 2027 residentials run between 28 June and 30 July 2027; the psychology week is to be confirmed.

Sutton Trust UK Summer Schools (psychology courses, e.g. Durham)

35applyY12 at state schools; criteria include first generation to university and free school meals

When: 2026 pattern: applications opened 12 January and closed 5 March (Cambridge courses 12 February). Confirm 2027 dates.

Details  summerschools.suttontrust.com/course/durham-university/psychology-3
