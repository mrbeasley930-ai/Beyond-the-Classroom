Sixth form · Art (Fine Art) · 2026-27

Art beyond the classroom36 things to make, see, read and enter. All free.

Art schools and foundation courses interview from your sketchbook, so everything here should end up in it: a drawing from a gallery visit, a response to a talk, an entry for a competition. The Birmingham galleries, the Courtauld and National Gallery programmes and the talks below are free; the competition deadlines are real. Each time you do something, write one line in your sketchbook: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Titus Kaphar: Can art amend history?

Competitions · 3 things

Enter

Ya

Young Artists' Summer Show (Royal Academy of Arts)

19entryAges 4–18 living and at school in the UK; one artwork per pupilvia school

Submit one piece of your own work, from any year, to a free national exhibition at the Royal Academy in London. More than 25,000 students took part in 2026. Selected work is shown in the gallery and online.

When: 2026 pattern: school registration and submissions 5 January – 4 March; outcomes by end of May; exhibition at the RA 14 July – 30 August. 2027 dates not yet published; confirm in December.

RA Young Artists  youngartists.royalacademy.org.uk/get-involved

Ap

Articulation Prize (National Gallery)

20entryAges 16–19enter yourselfNot stated on the site; confirm

A 10-minute talk to an audience about one work of art, building or artefact. Good practice for the written related study: you research one work closely and argue a view about it.

When: School heats first, then regional heats and finals at partner museums; 2026-27 dates not on the site when checked. Confirm.

Articulation Prize  nationalgallery.org.uk/learning/secondary-schools/articulation/articulation-prize

St

Sir Trenchard Cox Scholarship (The Arts Society and Art History Abroad)

21essayAges 17–18 (16–18 on the Arts Society page)enter yourself

Two 400-word essays, one on an artwork you love and one on an artwork you dislike, each with a photograph. First prize is a two-week art history course in Italy; flights, meals and insurance are not covered.

When: Entries open January 2027. 2026 deadline was 17.30 on 22 March, results 3 April; confirm the 2027 deadline.

AHA scholarships  arthistoryabroad.com/scholarships-awards

34applyY12 at UK state schools; priority to under-represented backgrounds

35applyY12 at state schools with at least five GCSEs at 9–6; priority criteria apply

When: Applications usually open in the new year; 2027 dates not published. Confirm.

Details  hoart.cam.ac.uk/outreach/sutton-trust-summer-school
