PE beyond the classroom32 things to do, watch, read and apply for. Free unless the entry says otherwise.

Sport science, physiotherapy and PE teaching courses want evidence of the science behind performance as well as the performance itself. The Physiological Society, Loughborough and Birmingham lectures and the podcasts below are the science; the placements and the Young Ambassadors and Level 3 programmes are the practical evidence. Record your own training and what the data shows. Each time you do something, write one line in your training diary: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

British Biology Olympiad (UK Biology Competitions)

30paperY12 and Y13via school

Two 45-minute online papers in school in January. A strong fit for anyone who likes the applied anatomy and physiology and exercise physiology parts of the course.

When: Online papers 15–22 January 2027. School registration opens 1 October 2026.

UKBC page  ukbiologycompetitions.org/british-biology-olympiad

Ks

Kelvin Sciences Essay Competition (Peterhouse, Cambridge)

31essayY12 (penultimate year), UK and Irelandenter yourself

A 2,000-word science essay on one of six set questions. The 2026 questions were biology, chemistry and physics based, so check whether one suits a PE and sport science interest before starting. No AI-generated content.

When: Questions released in the spring; 2026 deadline was 20 April, with teacher verification by 4 May. Confirm 2027 on the Peterhouse site.

Peterhouse  pet.cam.ac.uk/peterhouse-kelvin-biological-sciences-essay-competition

32entrySixth-form pupilsenter yourself

When: Open all year; no deadline published.

Hertford  hertford.ox.ac.uk/study-here/outreach/digital/challenge
