Sixth form · English Literature · 2026-27

English Literature beyond the classroom35 things to read, hear, watch and enter. All free.

English admissions turn on what you have read beyond the set texts and whether you can say something about it. Keep a commonplace book: a line you liked, where it came from, and what it does. The essay and poetry prizes below are where that reading goes. Each time you do something, write one line in your commonplace book: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Gp

Gould Prize for Essays in English Literature (Trinity College, Cambridge)

26essayY12 when questions are releasedenter yourself

A critical essay of 1,500–2,500 words on one of a set of questions. £600 first prize and £400 second prize, each split between you and the school. It must be your own work: no help from school and no AI.

When: Questions released in the spring term 2027; deadline expected end of July 2027 (2026 deadline was 31 July, 12 noon). Confirm on the Trinity page.

Trinity  trin.cam.ac.uk/undergraduate/essay-prizes/english-literature

Tc

Thomas Campion English Prize (Peterhouse, Cambridge)

27essayY12enter yourself

An English essay on a set question. £500 first prize, £250 second. Winners and highly commended entrants are invited to lunch at Peterhouse with academics.

When: Questions usually released in the new year; deadline in the spring (March–April in past rounds). Peterhouse had not published 2027 dates. Confirm.

Peterhouse  pet.cam.ac.uk/essay-prizes

Woolf Essay Prize (Newnham College, Cambridge)

28essayWomen in Y12enter yourself

An essay on literature, women's history and society, set in honour of Virginia Woolf's talk that became A Room of One's Own. Questions come out in February.

When: 2027 round opens February 2027 and closes June 2027 (2026 deadline was 8 June, 9am). Confirm exact dates.

Newnham  newn.cam.ac.uk/study-here/access-and-outreach/events-and-programmes/woolf-essay-prize-2026

Oy

Orwell Youth Prize 2027: "Acts of Courage" (Orwell Foundation)

29essayYears 7–13 at UK schools; Years 12–13 are a separate categoryenter yourself

Up to 1,500 words in any form (essay, poem, story, journalism) on the theme "Acts of Courage". Send a draft by 3 March and you get written feedback before the final deadline.

When: Open now. Optional feedback deadline Wednesday 3 March 2027; final deadline Wednesday 28 April 2027.

Orwell Youth Prize  orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-youth-prize/about/enter-the-orwell-youth-prize

Fy

Foyle Young Poets of the Year Award (Poetry Society)

30paperAged 11–17 on the closing date, so Y12 and younger Y13s onlyenter yourself

Send up to three original poems. 100 winners each year (15 top winners, 85 commended), with Poetry Society membership, publication and mentoring. AI-written poems are banned.

When: 2026 round closed 31 July 2026; winners are announced in the autumn. 2027 round expected to open in the spring and close 31 July 2027. Confirm.

Foyle Young Poets  foyleyoungpoets.org/rules

Betty Haigh Shakespeare Prize (English Association)

31essayAges 11–19 in school, college or sixth formenter yourself

A 1,500-word essay comparing a scene from a Shakespeare tragedy with a film or TV adaptation of it. Othello works well.

When: The last round we could confirm opened in June 2025 and closed 30 September 2025. Check the English Association page now for a 2026 round; otherwise expect a summer 2027 opening. Confirm.

English Association  englishassociation.ac.uk/the-betty-haigh-shakespeare-prize
