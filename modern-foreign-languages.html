Sixth form · Modern Foreign Languages · 2026-27

Languages beyond the classroom40 things to watch, listen to, read and enter. All free.

Language degrees want to know what you watch, read and listen to in the language, and whether you have read anything written for native speakers. The institutes, news sites and podcasts below are free; the Anthea Bell translation prize, the Linguistics Olympiad and the essay competitions are where you prove it. Keep a diary in the language, even a line a day. Each time you do something, write one line in your language diary: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

29paperAll ages; Advanced level suits Y12–13. No knowledge of the languages in the problems is needed.via school

Puzzles in which you work out the grammar of a language you have never seen from a few examples. Good practice for the reasoning in translation.

When: Round 1 Advanced: entry deadline 2 November 2026, sitting 9–13 November 2026, results 7 December 2026. Round 2 on 3 February 2027 for those invited. Top scorers go on to training for the International Linguistics Olympiad.
