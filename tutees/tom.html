Six heads Open a card, do the thing, then press "Done — write it up". Every entry is filed under the UCAS question it will answer. Q1 why this subject · Q2 how your studies have prepared you · Q3 what you have done outside education.

The book Every entry you have written, newest first. The tracker on Mr Beasley's side shows these in full.

The statement workbench UCAS asks three questions; the three answers share 4,000 characters, with at least 350 in each. Draft here from your entries. Oxford's own advice: tutors would rather talk about one thing you read in depth than hear a list of titles.
