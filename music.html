Sixth form · Music · 2026-27

Music beyond the classroom39 things to hear, play, read and enter. All free.

Conservatoires and music departments ask what you listen to and what you have performed or written outside school. The recordings, broadcasts and scores below are free; the youth ensembles and composition competitions are the ways to play and write. Keep a listening log: the piece, the performer, one thing you noticed. Each time you do something, write one line in your listening log: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Talks and lectures · 16 things

Watch

Sc

Betsy Schwarm: Why should you listen to Vivaldi's "Four Seasons"?

124mTED-Ed

A 4 min talk (TED-Ed).

Think about: Compare the ritornello structure she describes in Spring with the first movement of Il Gardellino. What does the soloist do that the tutti does not?

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/betsy_schwarm_why_should_you_listen_to_vivaldi_s_four_seasons

Br

David Byrne: How architecture helped music evolve

1316mtalk

A 16 min talk.

Think about: Byrne says the room shapes the music. Take one AoS1 set work: where was it first heard, and what in its scoring or texture suits that space?

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/david_byrne_how_architecture_helped_music_evolve

Mr

Elizabeth Hellmuth Margulis: Why we love repetition in music

144mTED-Ed

A 4 min talk (TED-Ed).

Think about: Find three different uses of repetition in a Chopin, Brahms or Grieg set work and say what each one does for the listener.

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/elizabeth_hellmuth_margulis_why_we_love_repetition_in_music

Rn

Mark Ronson: How sampling transformed music

1517mtalk

A 17 min talk.

Think about: Choose a track by Daft Punk or Beyoncé that uses a sample. What does the new track change about the original, and why?

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/mark_ronson_how_sampling_transformed_music

Kirby Ferguson: Embrace the remix

169mtalk

A 9 min talk.

Think about: Bach arranged Vivaldi concertos for keyboard. Is that a remix? Say where borrowing becomes copying. This talk is there to be argued with.

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/kirby_ferguson_embrace_the_remix

Competitions · 5 things

Enter

By

BBC Young Composer Competition (BBC Radio 3 / BBC Proms)

28paperAges 12–18, UK. Three categories: Lower Junior (12–14), Upper Junior (15–16), Senior (17–18).enter yourself

Send in a piece you have written, in any style. Winners get a mentor composer and write a piece for the BBC Concert Orchestra. Your NEA composition could be the starting point.

When: Runs every other year. The 2025 round was open March to September 2025, with winners announced in December. The next round is expected in 2027; confirm dates on the BBC page.

BBC Young Composer  bbc.co.uk/programmes/articles/4mY3MbSJ5G2LvSbzZPbc85G/bbc-young-composer

Ny

NCEM Young Composers Award (National Centre for Early Music with BBC Radio 3)

29paperUK residents up to 25; judged in two categories, 18 and under, and 19–25.enter yourself

Write a 3–4 minute piece for a named early-music group. The 2026 brief was soprano, cornetto and harpsichord or organ. Winners are performed live and broadcast on Radio 3's Early Music Show.

When: The 2026 winners' pieces are premiered by The Gonzaga Band at Royal Birmingham Conservatoire, Tuesday 27 October 2026, lunchtime. The 2027 brief is not yet published; in the 2026 round registration closed on 2 February and the final was in York in April. Confirm.

NCEM award  youngcomposersaward.co.uk

Nt

National Youth Orchestra of Great Britain: auditions

30paperAges 13–18 on 1 September of the application year; usually around Grade 8 standard, no exam certificates needed.enter yourself

Free to apply. If you get in, there is a fee, but bursaries cover it for families who need them.

When: For the Orchestra of 2027, applications closed 17 May 2026, video auditions were due 31 May, and live auditions ran 1–13 September 2026 in London, Birmingham and Manchester. The 2028 round is expected to follow the same pattern; confirm.

NYO  nyo.org.uk/get-involved/the-nyo-orchestra

Nn

National Youth Choir (15–18 choir): auditions

31entrySingers in Y11–13 who were aged 15, 16 or 17 on 31 August 2026 (a separate choir takes 18–25). UK citizens or residents.enter yourself

A free 10–15 minute online audition. Your parent books it by 9am on 6 October 2026. Members go on residential courses in the holidays; there is a fee, with help for families on lower incomes.

When: Booking window closes 9am, Tuesday 6 October 2026. Online auditions over Zoom, 17 October to 1 November 2026. First course in spring 2027. Next booking window expected September 2027.

NYC auditions  nationalyouthchoir.org.uk/how-do-i-join-national-youth-choir

Cp

CBSO Project Remix 2027

32entryInstrumentalists aged 11–18 at around Grade 5 or above; no exam certificates needed.enter yourself

A free CBSO ensemble playing new arrangements of dance-based music, coached by orchestra members, with a concert at Symphony Hall. Apply by 8 November.

When: Apply by Sunday 8 November 2026. Rehearsals on weekday evenings (mostly Tuesdays) at the CBSO Centre from January 2027, a tutti day on Sunday 7 March, and a concert at Symphony Hall on Sunday 14 March 2027.

Project Remix  cbso.co.uk/take-part/young-people/project-remix
