Sixth form · Latin · 2026-27

Latin beyond the classroom36 things to translate, read, hear and enter. All free.

Latin applicants are asked what they have read beyond the set texts, in Latin and in translation, and whether they can read a line aloud with the metre. The translation and reading competitions below are the proof. The commentaries and dictionaries are the tools; the podcasts and lectures are where the arguments are. Each time you do something, write one line in your commentary: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

This half term

Wanted by Friday, 23 October 2026

Three picks from the department for this half term. Do them, and write a line in the notebook for each.

Io
Listen · In Our Time: The Aeneid

Listen to In Our Time on the Aeneid (45 min) and note two points you could use in an essay on Aeneid 4.

Hp
Hypotactic · Hypotactic

Scan ten lines of Aeneid 4 yourself, then check them on Hypotactic and read them aloud. It is practice for the scansion question and for the ARLT reading competition.

Join · Classics for All: The Chorus

Join The Chorus, Classics for All's free network for 16–25s, and go to one online meeting this term.

Peta Greenfield and Alex Gendler: History vs. Augustus

275mTED-Ed

A 5 min talk (TED-Ed).

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/peta_greenfield_alex_gendler_history_vs_augustus
