Sixth form · Economics · 2026-27

Economics beyond the classroom37 things to read, enter, listen to and watch. All free.

Economics is read more than it is watched. The best applicants follow the news through the FT, the Economist and the Bank of England, and can argue about it. The essay prizes below reward exactly that, and the podcasts and lectures give you the arguments. Each time you do something, write one line in your ledger: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Competitions · 6 things

Enter

Ye

Young Economist of the Year (Royal Economic Society, via Discover Economics; sponsored by KPMG)

09paperYears 10–13; individuals or groupsenter yourself

Answer one of five set questions as a blog (up to 1,000 words), a 5-minute video or podcast, or a short slide deck. 2026 questions included dynamic pricing, free trade and AI and growth. Winners have visited HM Treasury and 10 Downing Street.

When: 2026 round launched 23 March and closed 29 June; shortlist September, final October 2026. Expect a similar pattern in 2027: confirm.

Discover Economics  discovereconomics.co.uk/young-economist-of-the-year-2026

Fn

FCDO Next Generation Economics Competition (Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office with LSE)

10paperAges 14–18 at UK schools; Under 16 and Under 18 categoriesenter yourself

A letter of up to 1,000 words answering one of four questions on development and international economics (2026: protectionism, creative destruction, women's economic participation, sustainable development). Prizes up to £350; top eight visit the FCDO and LSE. Harvard referencing required; AI may not write the entry.

When: 2026 round opened 21 May and closed 28 June; results by September. GOV.UK says it returns in 2027: confirm dates.

GOV.UK  gov.uk/guidance/fcdo-next-generation-economics-competition

Go

Gerald O'Connell Essay Competition in Economics (Fitzwilliam College, Cambridge)

11entryY12 at UK state schools (home students)enter yourself

Up to 1,000 words, with a research log. 2026 asked you to describe a time market forces failed, estimate the losses and say what protected people. £200 first prize. No generative AI.

When: Questions published in the autumn; 2026 deadline was 6pm, 27 February. 2027 deadline expected late February: confirm.

Fitzwilliam  fitz.cam.ac.uk/fitzwilliam-college-essay-competitions-2026

Ms

Marshall Society Essay Competition (Cambridge University Economics Society)

12entryPre-university students; Y12 and Y13enter yourself

Up to 1,500 words on one of six questions (2026 included tariffs, central banks and energy shocks, and Britain leaving the gold standard in 1931). £100 first prize. AI may be used for research only and must be declared.

When: Runs over the summer; 2026 deadline was 30 August, results mid-September. 2027 dates: confirm.

Marshall Society  marshallsoc.org/marshall-society-essay-competition-2026

Oe

Oxford Economics Society Sixth Form Essay Competition

13entryY12 and Y13enter yourself

Up to 2,000 words on one of three questions (2026: oil shocks, whether the CMA should approve mergers more readily, lessons from 2008). £100 first prize.

When: 2026 deadline was 11.59pm, 14 August. 2027 dates: confirm.

Oxford Econ Soc  oxfordeconsoc.org/essay-competition

Ee

Entrepreneurship Essay Competition (King's Entrepreneurship Lab, King's College, Cambridge)

14entryUK Years 11–13enter yourself

Up to 1,000 words on one of four questions about entrepreneurship. £1,500 first prize, two runners-up at £750. No generative AI.

When: 2026 deadline was 9pm, 5 August; prize ceremony at King's in December 2026. 2027 dates: confirm.

King's E-Lab  kingselab.org/essay-competition
