Sixth form · Computer Science · 2026-27

Computer Science beyond the classroom41 things to build, solve, watch and enter. All free.

Computer science courses want evidence that you program outside lessons and that you can reason about problems on paper. The Bebras and Olympiad rounds test the second; CS50x, Advent of Code and the Raspberry Pi competition are the first. Keep what you build somewhere you can show it. Each time you do something, write one line in your dev log: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Competitions · 5 things

Enter

Bi

British Informatics Olympiad (BIO)

06paperUnder 19, full-time pre-university education in mainland Britain; mainly Y12 and Y13via school

A 3-hour paper of three problems, each solved by writing a program in any language. The best scorers go to a final in Cambridge, and the top four form the UK team for the International Olympiad in Informatics.

When: Round 1 set in school on any date in a winter window (2025-26: 8 December 2025 to 23 January 2026). Final in Cambridge at Easter (2026: 3–5 April). Confirm 2026-27 window on the BIO site.

olympiad.org.uk  olympiad.org.uk

Ub

UK Bebras Challenge (Elite group)

07entryElite group: Y12 and Y13 (ages 16–19)via school

45 minutes of computational thinking problems online, marked automatically. Try the past challenges first; no login needed.

When: 9–20 November 2026 (published). 45 minutes, sat in school at any time in the window.

bebras.uk  bebras.uk

Rp

Raspberry Pi Foundation Coding Challenge (formerly the Oxford University Computing Challenge)

08entryPupils aged 10+ at schools that took part in the preceding UK Bebras Challengevia school

A 45-minute coding challenge, block-based or text-based, at two difficulty levels. You can only enter if the school has done Bebras first.

When: Follows Bebras; the OUCC ran in January in earlier years. Date for 2027 not published; confirm through the Bebras teacher account.

Coding Challenge  bebras.uk/index.php?action=content&id=132

Nc

National Cipher Challenge (University of Southampton)

09entryUK school pupils, individually or in teamsenter yourself

A series of codes to break online over the autumn term, getting harder each week. Good practice for the encryption content of Component 01.

When: Registration opened 24 September 2026; challenges run to January 2027; prize-giving at Bletchley Park in March 2027.

cipherchallenge.org  cipherchallenge.org

Pc

Perse Coding Team Challenge: Sixth Form Gold Rush

10paperSixth form, solo. The main PCTC team rounds are for Y11 and below.via school

50 minutes, on your own, on six questions taken from the Round 2 paper. Certificates only.

When: 15–19 March 2027 (published), straight after PCTC Round 2 (1–12 March 2027).

PCTC  pctc.perse.co.uk
