Maths beyond the classroom39 problems to try, talks to watch, things to read and enter. Free unless the entry says otherwise.

Maths beyond the classroom means solving problems that take longer than ten minutes. Most of what is here is problems: the UKMT challenges and their past papers, the STEP and MAT questions that the top universities set, and sites that mark you instantly. The talks and reading show where the subject goes after A level. Each time you do something, write one line in your rough book: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Competitions · 5 things

Enter

Us

UKMT Senior Mathematical Challenge (SMC)

06paperY13 and below; aimed at Y12 and Y13enter yourself£15 per 10 papers (download and print); £16 per 10 posted

90 minutes: 22 multiple-choice questions and three questions with a three-digit answer (new format for 2026). High scorers are invited to the Senior Kangaroo or the British Mathematical Olympiad.

When: Wednesday 7 October 2026. Deadline for downloadable papers is the same day; the posted-paper deadline (16 September) has passed.

UKMT SMC  ukmt.org.uk/senior-challenges/senior-mathematical-challenge

Bm

British Mathematical Olympiad Round 1 and Round 2 (UKMT), with the Senior Kangaroo

07entryY13 and below. About 1,000 top SMC scorers qualify for BMO1; several thousand more for the Senior Kangaroo.via schoolBMO1 free to qualifiers; £40 per discretionary entry. Check the UKMT handbook for Kangaroo fees.

BMO1 is three and a half hours on six problems, with full written proofs. The Kangaroo is 60 minutes, 20 questions with whole-number answers. Top BMO1 scorers go on to BMO2.

When: BMO1 and Senior Kangaroo: Wednesday 18 November 2026. BMO2: Wednesday 20 January 2027.

UKMT BMO1  ukmt.org.uk/senior-challenges/british-maths-olympiad-round-1

Uk

UKMT Senior Team Mathematical Challenge

08paperY12 and Y13; team of four with no more than two Y13via school£50 per team

A team competition in rounds. Four of you represent the school at a regional final.

When: Regional finals across the UK between 3 and 30 November 2026; registration opened 10 September and closes three weeks before each event. National final March 2027 (confirm venue).

UKMT STMC  ukmt.org.uk/team-challenges/senior-team-mathematical-challenge
