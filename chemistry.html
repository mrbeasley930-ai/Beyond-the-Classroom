Competitions · 4 things

Enter

Ol

UK Chemistry Olympiad

012hY12–13via schoollate Jan · check

A two-hour paper of problems well beyond A level, sat in school in late January. Your teacher registers the school; you cannot enter on your own.

Think about: Do a past paper first (tile Op) so the style is not a shock.

RSC Olympiad page  edu.rsc.org/enrichment/uk-chemistry-olympiad

Cc

Cambridge Chemistry Challenge (C3L6)

0290mY12via schoolJune · check

A 90-minute paper sat in school, usually in June. A gold award gets you an invitation to a residential at the University of Cambridge at the end of August.

Think about: Past papers are on the same site (tile Cx).

c3l6.com  c3l6.com

Kv

Kelvin Science Prize, Peterhouse

03essayY12enter yourselfspring · check

A science essay on a question set by Peterhouse, Cambridge. Questions come out in the spring; the 2026 deadline was 20 April. Winners are invited to lunch with Cambridge academics.

Think about: Pick the question you would still want to answer after three weeks of reading about it.

Peterhouse  pet.cam.ac.uk/peterhouse-kelvin-biological-sciences-essay-competition

Ox

The Oxford Scientist writing competition

045hall yearsenter yourself

A short piece of science writing for the magazine Oxford students run. Check the site for this year's window.

Think about: Write about one piece of chemistry you have met this year for someone who has never studied it.

oxsci.org  oxsci.org

Talks and lectures · 15 things

Watch

Mo

Miguel A. Modestino: How to transform the chemical industry, one reaction at a time

059mTED

A TED talk on running industrial reactions with electricity instead of heat.

Think about: Which reaction in the A level organic course would you most want to run electrochemically, and what would it save?

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/miguel_a_modestino_how_to_transform_the_chemical_industry_one_reaction_at_a_time

Va

Vaitea Cowan: How green hydrogen could end the fossil fuel era

069mTED

A TED talk on mass-producing electrolysers that split water into hydrogen and oxygen.

Think about: Write the electrolysis half-equations and explain where the energy cost sits.

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/vaitea_cowan_how_green_hydrogen_could_end_the_fossil_fuel_era

Ob

Karin Öberg: The galactic recipe for a living planet

0713mTED

A TED talk on astrochemistry: the molecules that form in space before planets do.

Think about: Which of the molecules named have you met in the course, and what evidence identifies them at that distance?

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/karin_oberg_the_galactic_recipe_for_a_living_planet

Hy

Christy L. Haynes: Can nanoparticles help fight hunger?

0811mTED

A TED talk on using nanoparticles to protect crops.

Think about: What changes chemically when a material is made at nanoscale, and why does that matter for a plant?

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/christy_l_haynes_can_nanoparticles_help_fight_hunger

Vo

Normand Voyer: Are life-saving medicines hiding in the world's coldest places?

0912mTED

A TED talk on finding new drug molecules in organisms from polar regions.

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/normand_voyer_are_life_saving_medicines_hiding_in_the_world_s_coldest_places

Sam Afoullouss: The deep sea's medicinal secrets

1013mTED

A TED talk on searching deep-sea organisms for new medicines.

Think about: What is the case for and against collecting organisms from an environment we know little about?

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/sam_afoullouss_the_deep_sea_s_medicinal_secrets

Clarice Phelps: How I claimed a seat at the periodic table

1115mTED

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/clarice_phelps_how_i_claimed_a_seat_at_the_periodic_table
