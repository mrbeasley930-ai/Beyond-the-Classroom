Sixth form · Politics · 2026-27

Politics beyond the classroom39 things to read, listen to, watch and enter. Free unless the entry says otherwise.

Politics applicants are judged on how well they follow events and how fairly they argue. Hansard, TheyWorkForYou, Pew and the podcasts here give you the material; the essay prizes, Debating Matters and the Schools’ Mace give you the practice. Read opinions you disagree with. Each time you do something, write one line in your diary: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Talks and lectures · 14 things

Watch

Li

Eric Liu: Why ordinary people need to understand power

1317mtalk

A 17 min talk.

Think about: Paper 1 democracy and participation: take his definition of power and apply it to one UK pressure group. Where does that group's power come from?

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/eric_liu_why_ordinary_people_need_to_understand_power

Hd

Jonathan Haidt: The moral roots of liberals and conservatives

1418mtalk

A 18 min talk.

Think about: Paper 3 core ideologies: which of his moral foundations fit traditional conservatism, and which fit the New Right? Where does his picture of liberals not match classical liberalism?

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/jonathan_haidt_the_moral_roots_of_liberals_and_conservatives

Bt

Alexander Betts: Why Brexit happened -- and what to do next

1517mtalk

A 17 min talk.

Think about: Paper 1 elections and referendums, and voting behaviour: what social divisions does he say explain the 2016 result? Test one against the referendum data by age or education.

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/alexander_betts_why_brexit_happened_and_what_to_do_next

Sn

Michael Sandel: The lost art of democratic debate

1619mtalk

A 19 min talk.

Think about: Paper 2 Supreme Court: he uses PGA Tour v. Martin. Was the Court interpreting the law or making it? Link your answer to judicial activism and restraint.

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/michael_sandel_the_lost_art_of_democratic_debate

Lawrence Lessig: We the People, and the Republic we must reclaim

1718mtalk

Watch on TED  ted.com/talks/lawrence_lessig_we_the_people_and_the_republic_we_must_reclaim

Competitions · 5 things

Enter

Ra

R.A. Butler Politics Prize (Trinity College and POLIS, Cambridge)

27essayY12 when questions are released (results expected Sept 2027–Aug 2028 for the 2027 round)enter yourself

Up to 3,000 words on one of about ten questions. 2026 questions covered strongmen and leadership, self-determination, climate displacement, war crimes, social media and globalisation. £600 first prize, split between you and the school. 1,040 entries in 2026.

When: Questions released March 2027 (POLIS says so). 2026 deadline was 12 noon, 31 July; 2027 deadline: confirm.

Trinity  trin.cam.ac.uk/undergraduate/essay-prizes/politics

Fa

FT-APG A Level Essay Competition (PSA American Politics Group with the FT Schools Programme)

28essayAges 16–19 studying American politics at A levelenter yourself

1,500 words (plus or minus 10%) on US politics from Paper 2: Congress, the presidency, the Supreme Court, civil rights. Best essay gets a £100 voucher, funded travel to the APG colloquium and possible publication on an FT blog; four further £50 prizes.

When: 2026 deadline was 16 March, with a free preparatory online event on 25 February. 2027 round: confirm.

PSA  psa.ac.uk/news/new-a-level-essay-competition

To

The Orwell Youth Prize 2027 (The Orwell Foundation)

29essayYears 7–13 in UK schools; Y12–13 up to 1,500 wordsenter yourself

Political writing in any form: essay, journalism, story, poem, play. Theme for 2027 is 'Acts of Courage'. Enter by 3 March to get personal feedback before the final deadline. Winners get £100 and Orwell's works for themselves and the school.

When: Open now. Theme 'Acts of Courage'. Feedback deadline 3 March 2027; final deadline 28 April 2027.

Orwell Foundation  orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-youth-prize/about/introduction

30entrySixth formenter yourselfNo fee stated on the site; confirm with the organiser.

When: Regional championships through the year; Transatlantic championship 14 November 2026 in London. No 2027 championships listed yet: confirm.

Debating Matters  debatingmatters.com

The long-running national schools debating competition, judged by ESU-trained judges. Ask your teacher about entering a team next year.
