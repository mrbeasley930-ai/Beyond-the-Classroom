Sixth form · Physics · 2026-27

Physics beyond the classroom41 things to watch, solve, read and enter. Free unless the entry says otherwise.

Physics admissions tutors look for two things beyond the grade: problems you have tried that were harder than the exam, and ideas you have followed further than the specification. The Olympiad and Senior Physics Challenge papers cover the first; the lectures, podcasts and reading here cover the second. Each time you do something, write one line in your lab book: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Competitions · 6 things

Enter

Bp

British Physics Olympiad: Round 0, Round 1 and Round 2 (BPhO)

01paperY13 and below; Y12 may enter Round 0 as a trial runvia schoolRound 0: £14 per school. Round 1: £18 per pupil, four free papers for non-fee-paying UK schools. Round 2 free.

The national physics olympiad. A multiple-choice first round in October decides who sits the two-hour problem paper in November. Round 0 uses Y12 topics such as circular motion, the photoelectric effect and capacitors.

When: Round 0 (1 hour, multiple choice, non-calculator): Thursday 1 October 2026. Round 1 (2 hours): Thursday 12 November 2026, by invitation from Round 0 results. Round 2 (3 hours, team selection): Thursday 21 January 2027, by invitation.

BPhO Round 1  bpho.org.uk/bpho/round-1.html

Bs

BPhO Senior Physics Challenge (Y12)

02paperY12via schoolOnline quiz: £20 per school entry. Paper: free.

A Y12 physics competition in two parts: a multiple-choice quiz in January and a one-hour problem paper in March. Certificates for all.

When: Online quiz (2 x 30 min) any time Monday 18 to Friday 22 January 2027. Paper competition (1 hour) Friday 5 March 2027.

BPhO SPC  bpho.org.uk/bpho/as-challenge.html

Bh

BPhO Physics Challenge (Y13)

03paperY13via school

A one-hour paper on Y13 physics, pushed into applications. Merit certificate at 20 out of 50.

When: Sat in school at any time from September to the entry deadline, Saturday 12 December 2026.

BPhO Physics Challenge  bpho.org.uk/bpho/a2-challenge.html

Ba

British Astronomy and Astrophysics Olympiad: Astro Challenge and Astro Round 1 (BAAO)

04paperY13 and below; suits pupils taking the AQA Astrophysics optionvia schoolAstro Challenge free. Astro Round 1: £15 per pupil, four free papers for non-fee-paying schools.

The astronomy olympiad. The Astro Challenge is a one-hour paper mixing astronomy and A level physics; Round 1 is harder and includes an observational astronomy question.

When: Astro Challenge (1 hour) any time up to Saturday 12 December 2026. Astro Round 1 (2 hours) Monday 23 to Friday 27 November 2026. Round 2 (by invitation) Tuesday 26 January 2027.

BAAO Astro Challenge  bpho.org.uk/baao/astro-challenge

Ks

Kelvin Sciences Essay Competition (Peterhouse, Cambridge)

05essayY12 (penultimate year), UK and Irelandenter yourself

An essay of up to 2,000 words on one of six science questions, some of them physics. Winners share a £750 prize pool and go to a prizegiving in Cambridge.

When: 2026 questions included quantum computing and biosignatures on exoplanets; 2026 deadline was 20 April, teacher verification by 4 May. Confirm 2027 dates on the Peterhouse site.

Peterhouse  pet.cam.ac.uk/peterhouse-kelvin-sciences-essay-competition

Bc

BPhO Computational Challenge

06entryY11, Y12 and Y13enter yourself

Learn to model physics in Excel or Python through weekly online talks, then submit a project. No coding experience needed.

When: Project runs April to August 2027. In 2026 a ten-week online course (Thursday evenings, from 8 January) came first; confirm the 2027 course dates.

Computational Challenge  bpho.org.uk/bpho/computational-challenge
