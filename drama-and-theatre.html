Sixth form · Drama and Theatre · 2026-27

Drama and Theatre beyond the classroom38 things to see, do, read and enter. All free.

Drama schools audition and universities interview, and both ask what you have seen and what you have made. The National Theatre Collection, the RSC and Old Vic resources and the cheap-ticket schemes below are ways to see productions; the Rep, Old Vic and Frantic Assembly programmes and the playwriting prize are ways to make work. Write down what you saw and one choice the director made. Each time you do something, write one line in your rehearsal diary: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Competitions · 3 things

Enter

Yp

Young Playwrights Award (Royal Court Theatre)

19entryAny teenager in the UKenter yourself

Write a play on any subject and send it in. Winning plays are published by Nick Hern Books. The Royal Court runs free workshops and video masterclasses to help.

When: 2026 entries were open 27 January – 22 April 2026. Expect a similar window in 2027; confirm on the Royal Court site in January.

Royal Court  royalcourttheatre.com/write-and-create/schools-young-people/young-playwrights-award

Gp

Gould Prize for Essays in English Literature (Trinity College, Cambridge)

20essayY12 (Lower Sixth)enter yourself

A critical essay of 1,500–2,500 words. Plays written in English count, so a pupil could write on Churchill, Williams or Butterworth. £600 first prize, split between you and the school. No creative writing and no AI.

When: 2027 details to be posted in February 2027; the 2026 deadline was 31 July, 12 noon. Confirm.

Trinity  trin.cam.ac.uk/undergraduate/essay-prizes/english-literature

Tc

Thomas Campion English Prize (Peterhouse, Cambridge)

21essayY12enter yourself

An English essay on a set question; check each year whether a question fits a play you study. Winners are invited to a lunch at Peterhouse with academics.

When: Questions released in the spring; deadline usually March–April. Confirm on Peterhouse site.

Peterhouse  pet.cam.ac.uk/essay-prizes-faqs
