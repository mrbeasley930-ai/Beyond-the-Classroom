Station 7 · the ticker-timer · new stories every morning

Physics in the news

This week's physics stories from eleven sources, collected automatically every morning: the BBC, The Conversation, Quanta, Physics World, Science News, CERN, ESA, NASA, the American Physical Society's Physics magazine and Phys.org. Read one, open the full story, then press Log it and write two sentences: what was found, and the physics behind it. It saves to the department and goes into your lab book. Each story is tagged to the parts of the AQA course it seems to touch. The tags come from keywords, so some will be wrong; finding a better link to the course is part of the exercise.

What the department would see

A separate teacher page, no setup beyond pasting a token once: every pupil who has signed in; who tapped I want to enter for each competition (your entry list); how many of the half-term picks each pupil has done; each pupil's lab book as copyable text for references and personal statements; who on your form list has not signed in yet; a CSV. Content is updated each half term by editing one block at the top of the file; pupils keep everything they have done.
