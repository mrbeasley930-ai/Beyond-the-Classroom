Economics · The Tenth Chair · demo

A demo of a web page for your sixth-form economists.

It is a working copy of the page a pupil would use. The Bank's Monetary Policy Committee has nine members and makes eight decisions a year; the page adds a tenth chair. Before each decision the pupil reads the same releases the committee reads (CPI, labour market, GDP, the Bank's own forecast, all linked to the live ONS and Bank pages), votes, and writes their reasons. After the announcement the page shows how the committee split. Around that sits the briefing pack: 53 free things to enter, watch, listen to, read, do and apply for, every one tagged to an Edexcel A theme, with the primary data and texts first. Everything saves to the department, and there is a teacher dashboard that shows the votes and the reasoning.

What the department would see

A separate teacher page, with no setup beyond pasting a token once: every pupil who has signed in; each pupil's vote for every MPC decision with their written reasons, and whether they landed with the majority once the result is in; who tapped I want to enter for each prize (your list for Fitzwilliam, which caps entries per school); how many of the half-term agenda each pupil has done; how much of the pack each has read and which sections they have not opened; each set of minutes as copyable text for references and personal statements; who on your form list has not signed in yet; a CSV. After each decision you enter the result in one line and every pupil's chair is compared. The screens are updated by editing one block at the top of the file; pupils keep everything they have done.
