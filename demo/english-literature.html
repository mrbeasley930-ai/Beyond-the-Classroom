English Literature · The Lighthouse · demo

A demo of a web page for your English Literature sixth form.

It is a working copy of the page a pupil would use: 68 free things to read, listen to, do, watch, enter and apply for, each tagged to a part of AQA 7712 (Love through the ages, WW1 and its aftermath, Modern times, the NEA). It is laid out as a lighthouse at night. Each thing a pupil does lights a window on its floor, and the beam reaches further out to sea as they do more. What they read and think goes in a keeper's log, which the department can read. Every historical claim on the page carries its source and a label.

The keeper's log

Write up the watch

Keepers wrote up every watch in the log: the time, the weather, what the light did. Do the same for anything you watch, hear or read. Copy the line that made you stop, say which of your texts it speaks to, and what it showed you. A log like this is where your coursework ideas, an essay prize entry and your personal statement come from.

What the department would see

A separate teacher page, with no setup beyond pasting a token once: every pupil who has signed in; who tapped I want to enter for each prize (your list for the Campion Prize and Poetry by Heart, which go through school, and a heads-up on everyone entering the Keats-Shelley, Tower, Orwell and Cambridge prizes on their own); how many of the half-term orders each pupil has done; how far each pupil's light reaches and which floors are still dark; each pupil's log in full, as copyable text for references, personal statements and NEA conversations; who on your form list has not signed in yet; a CSV. Content is updated each half term by editing one block at the top of the file; pupils keep everything they have done.

Anything on this page can be changed for English: the items, the set-text tags, the half-term orders, the dates, the keeper's lines, the log fields, what gets tracked, the look of it. Use the Feedback button to say what you would want.
