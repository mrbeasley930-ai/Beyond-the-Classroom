Biology field course · demo

Hello. This is a demo of a web page for your sixth-form biologists.

It is a working copy of the page a pupil would use: 35 free things to read, practise, listen to, enter, watch and apply for, laid out as a field course on a map with six habitats. I am the field leader and I walk them round. Their progress is their own index of diversity, so spreading across habitats scores higher than doing ten of one thing. Everything they do saves to the department, and there is a teacher dashboard so you can see who has done what.

  1. Sign the register with any form and your own name. Nothing here reaches real pupils.
  2. Take the short tour that starts straight after, or skip it and click about.
  3. Press Feedback (bottom left) and tell Simon what you would want more of, less of, or changed.

Dates to know

The field season

The biology year from October to July. Competitions are entered through school: open one and tap I want to enter, and your name goes straight to the department. Dates marked check follow last year's pattern and have not been confirmed for 2027 yet.

One entry per thing

Your field notebook

What you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. Bring this to any conversation about university applications.

Your notebook saves to the department as you go. Copy my notebook gives you the text for a personal statement draft.

Field notebook

What the department would see

A separate teacher page, with no setup beyond pasting a token once: every pupil who has signed in; who tapped I want to enter for each competition (your entry list, which matters for the Olympiad because pupils cannot enter alone); how many of the half-term picks each pupil has done; each pupil's index of diversity and which habitats they have not visited; each field notebook as copyable text for references and personal statements; who on your form list has not signed in yet; a CSV. Content is updated each half term by editing one block at the top of the file; pupils keep everything they have done.

Anything on this page can be changed for Biology: the habitats, the picks, the dates, the field leader's lines, what gets tracked. Use the Feedback button to say what you would want.
