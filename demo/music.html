REA SINFONIA · THE STAND

7 · The programme · new every morning

This week's music

Two lists, collected automatically every morning. Broadcasts: this week's episodes of Composer of the Week, Record Review, Music Matters, The Early Music Show and Jazz Record Requests on Radio 3, and Add to Playlist and Soul Music on Radio 4, each with a link to listen on BBC Sounds. Stories: music news and reviews from the Guardian (classical, jazz, musicals and the main music desk), OperaWire, WhatsOnStage, The Arts Desk, Pitchfork, NME, Folk Radio UK and MusicRadar. Listen to or read one, then press Heard it and write two sentences: what you heard or read, and the musical feature behind it (texture, harmony, tonality, rhythm, timbre, structure, word-setting). It saves to the department and goes onto a programme note. Each item is tagged to the areas of study it seems to touch. The tags come from keywords, so some will be wrong; finding a better link to the course is part of the exercise.

Programme notes

Write the programme note

Every concert programme has a note on each piece: what it is, what to listen for, and what the writer noticed. Write one for anything you hear, watch, read, make or enter: what it was, the musical feature in one line, what you noticed, and what you would listen to next. These notes are where a personal statement, a conservatoire interview and a university essay come from.

What the department would see

A separate teacher page, with no setup beyond pasting a token once: every pupil who has signed in; who tapped I want to enter or I'm interested for each competition, audition and placement (your list for Project Remix, the Rotary heat, a Music for Youth group, and a heads-up on everyone applying to NYO, NYC or a conservatoire on their own); how much of the half-term schedule each pupil has done; each pupil's bars filled and which of the six sections they have used; every broadcast and story each pupil has logged from the programme, with the two sentences they wrote; each pupil's programme notes in full, as copyable text for references and personal statements; who on your form list has not signed in yet; a CSV. The schedule is edited in one block at the top of the file; the programme updates itself; pupils keep everything they have done.
