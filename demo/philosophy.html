Philosophy · The Walk · demo

A demo of a web page for your sixth-form philosophers.

It is a working copy of the page a pupil would use: 48 free things to enter, watch, listen to, read, do and apply for, laid out as a footpath in six stretches, each a place a philosopher is known to have walked. The pupil inks the route in as they go; Atma, Schopenhauer's poodle, keeps count and fetches things. Every anecdote on the page carries its source and a label (attested, reported, legend). Everything a pupil does saves to the department, and there is a teacher dashboard so you can see who has done what.

The walker's notebook

One entry per waymark

Date it. Say what it was. One thing you took from it, and the question it left you with. A notebook like this is where a personal statement, a Think essay and a reference come from; nobody can write those for you from a list of ticks.

What the department would see

A separate teacher page, with no setup beyond pasting a token once: every pupil who has signed in; who tapped I want to enter for each prize (your entry list for the Ethics Cup, PERC and #PhilosophyMatters, which go through school); how many of the half-term route card each pupil has done; how far along the route each has walked and which stretches they have not touched; each notebook as copyable text for references and personal statements; who on your form list has not signed in yet; a CSV. Content is updated each half term by editing one block at the top of the file; pupils keep everything they have done.

Anything on this page can be changed for Philosophy: the six stretches and their sources, the route card, the dates, Atma's lines, the AQA tagging, what gets tracked, the look of it. Use the Feedback button to say what you would want.
