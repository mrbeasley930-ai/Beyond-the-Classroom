Art · The Underpass · demo

A demo of a web page for your sixth-form artists.

It is a working copy of the page a pupil would use: 37 free things to enter, watch, listen to, read, do and apply for, laid out as one wall in an underpass with six parts. The pupil paints the wall as they go, and a magpie keeps track and fetches things for them. Everything they do saves to the department, and there is a teacher dashboard so you can see who has done what.

  1. Sign in with any form and your own name. Nothing here reaches real pupils.
  2. Take the short tour that starts straight after, or skip it and click about.
  3. Press Feedback (bottom left) and tell Simon what you would want more of, less of, or changed.

Any changes you would like, or anything that is wrong, send them to Simon.

What the department would see

A separate teacher page, with no setup beyond pasting a token once: every pupil who has signed in; who tapped I want to enter for each competition (your entry list for the RA show and Articulation, which go through school); how many of the half-term brief each pupil has done; how much of the wall each has painted and which parts are still grey; each black book as copyable text for references, personal statements and sketchbook annotation; who on your form list has not signed in yet; a CSV. Content is updated each half term by editing one block at the top of the file; pupils keep everything they have done.

Anything on this page can be changed for Art: the six parts of the wall, the brief, the dates, the magpie's lines, what gets tracked, the look of it. Use the Feedback button to say what you would want.
