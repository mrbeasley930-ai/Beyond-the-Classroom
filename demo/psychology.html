Psychology · The Participant Pool · demo

A demo of a web page for your Psychology sixth form

It is a working copy of the page a pupil would use: 65 free things to watch, listen to, read, do, enter and apply for, each tagged to a part of AQA 7182, pinned to a department noticeboard as recruitment flyers with tear-off tabs. In room G.14 pupils take part in short versions of four classic studies (Stroop, digit span, serial position, Loftus and Palmer). Each has consent, a debrief, the original finding, how well it has replicated, and three method questions. Their results come to you, so the class has its own data to analyse.

  1. Sign up with any form and your own name. Nothing here reaches real pupils.
  2. Take the short tour that starts straight after, or skip it. All four studies are open in this demo: try one.
  3. Open the teacher dashboard to see the class's data, then press Feedback (bottom left) and tell Simon what you would want.

The Participant PoolPsychology · Beyond the classroom

Free things for A-level Psychology: talks, lectures, podcasts, reading, competitions, and the way into a psychology degree. They are pinned to the noticeboard as recruitment flyers, one for each kind of thing. Every flyer has a tear-off tab for each item: do it and the tab comes off. In room G.14 you can take part in short versions of real experiments from the course, and see how your results compare with the original study. Ruth, the research assistant, will show you round.

Participant information

  1. What you do on this page (tabs torn off, lab book entries, study results and your answers) saves to the Psychology department, with your name and form. Your teacher can see it.
  2. When the class results of a study are shown to everyone, they have no names in them.
  3. You can withdraw from any study, during it or after it, and your data from that study is deleted. You keep the credits.
  4. Everything here is free to use. Links go to other websites, which have their own rules.

Lab book

Write it up

For anything you watch, hear or read: what it was, the finding in one line, what you made of it, and how you would test it. Psychologists write things down the same day. These entries are where a personal statement, an interview answer and a competition entry come from.

What the department would see

A separate teacher page, with no setup beyond pasting a token once. It shows every pupil who has signed up; the class's results from each study in G.14, as charts and as a CSV for JASP or Excel, with a button that writes the class results block for the pupil page; every pupil's answers to the method questions; who tapped I want to enter for each competition; which flyers and parts of the course get used; and each pupil's lab book, copyable for references.

Anything on this page can be changed for Psychology: the items, the course tags, which studies run and when, the method questions, the dates, Ruth's lines, what gets tracked, the look of it. Use the Feedback button to say what you would want.
