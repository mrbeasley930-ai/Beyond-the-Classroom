Maths and Further Maths · Hotel Hilbert · demo

A demo of a web page for your Maths and Further Maths sixth form.

It is a working copy of the page a pupil would use: 65 free things to read, listen to, watch, do, enter and apply for, each tagged to a part of the course, with 15 of them in a Further Maths wing (Core Pure, Further Statistics, the admissions tests). It is laid out as Hilbert's hotel, which is full and always has room for one more. Each thing a pupil does puts the light on in its room. There is a problem on the reception desk every week: pupils hand in an answer and their working, and the department can read it. Every historical claim on the page carries its source and a label.

  1. Sign in with any form and your own name. Nothing here reaches real pupils.
  2. Take the short tour that starts straight after, or skip it and click about.
  3. Press Feedback (bottom left) and tell Simon what you would want more of, less of, or changed, and anything you would correct.

Hotel Hilbertinfinitely many rooms, every one taken, and always room for you

Free things for A-level Maths and Further Maths: competitions, lectures and talks, podcasts, books, problems to work on, and the way into a maths degree. The page is laid out as a hotel. Each floor is one kind of thing, and each thing is a room; do it and the light goes on in its room. There is a problem on the reception desk every week, and hotel notepaper for anything you work out. The night porter will show you round. Everything you do saves to the department.

Write it down before it goes

Every room has a pad by the bed. Use it for anything you watch, hear, read or work out: what it was, the one idea worth keeping (an equation is fine), what you worked out from it, and what you still want to know. These pages are where a personal statement, an interview answer and an essay prize entry come from.
