Sixth form · Geography · 2026-27

Geography beyond the classroom39 things to watch, read, go to and enter. All free.

Geography stretches from glaciers to housing policy, and universities want to see which end you have followed and how far. The RGS lectures, the podcasts and the essay prizes below cover both. Fieldwork counts: note what you see on any trip, and what you would measure if you went back. Each time you do something, write one line in your field notebook: what you did, one thing you learned, one question it left you with. That is your personal statement material.

Competitions · 6 things

Enter

Yg

Young Geographer of the Year 2026 (Royal Geographical Society)

01entryKS5 category (ages 16–18); separate judging for state and independent schoolsenter yourself

Theme: "From Source to Sea". KS5 entrants make a poster or an Esri StoryMap on a river system: processes, landforms, and how people and nature use it. Links to Topic 2 (Landscape Systems) and Topic 5 (Water Cycle and Water Insecurity).

When: Deadline Friday 2 October 2026, 5pm. Awards ceremony in London, 27 November 2026.

RGS YGOTY  rgs.org/schools/competitions/young-geographer-of-the-year

Rs

RGS School Essay Competition 2026

02essayY12 and Y13 studying A level Geography; one entry per studententer yourself

Up to 1,000 words (or an ArcGIS StoryMap) on: "What factors drive migration, how are patterns shifting and what are the consequences?" You must cover climatic factors and post-2020 geopolitics and reference sources, including Financial Times articles (free through school). Fits Topic 8B (Migration, Identity and Sovereignty) and Topic 3 (Globalisation). Winner gets a £50 voucher and publication on the RGS site.

When: Deadline Monday 9 November 2026, 5pm. Results by 12 February 2027; awards in July 2027.

RGS essay  rgs.org/schools/competitions/school-essay-competition

Un

UK National Geography Olympiad (Geographical Association)

03entryPost-16 geographersvia school

An online subject-knowledge quiz sat in school. The highest scores nationally are passed to the contact for the International Geography Olympiad (iGeo 2027 is in Jelgava, Latvia, 2–9 August 2027).

When: First run: two-week window, Monday 9 – Friday 20 November 2026. Full instructions due from the GA this autumn.

GA Olympiad  geography.org.uk/uk-national-geography-olympiad

Cc

Christ Church Geography Essay Competition and Study Day (Oxford)

04essayY12 at UK state-maintained schools, studying A level Geographyenter yourself

An 800-word reflective essay that shows you thinking geographically (2026 theme: "The Age of Humans"). Selected entrants spend a free day at Christ Church with taster lectures and skills workshops.

When: 2026 deadline was Friday 17 April; study day was 27 May 2026. 2027 dates not yet published: confirm.

Christ Church  chch.ox.ac.uk/college/outreach/geography-study-day

Ron Cooke Award (Royal Geographical Society)

05entryA level students whose independent investigation (NEA) is nominated by their teacherenter yourself

An award for an outstanding independent investigation (9GE0/04). If your NEA is strong, your teacher may nominate it.

When: 2027 award open now; deadline Friday 12 March 2027, 5pm.

Ron Cooke  rgs.org/schools/competitions/ron-cooke-award

Rn

Rex Nettleford Essay Prize: Colonialism and its Legacies (Oriel College, Oxford)

06entryY12 at a UK school or collegeenter yourself

Up to 2,500 words on one of four questions about the legacies of colonialism, from any discipline. A geographer can answer through development, inequality or global governance (Topics 7, 8A and 8B). Two prizes of £250.

When: 2026 deadline was 12pm, 23 February 2026, with three webinars in January. 2027 dates not yet published: confirm.

Oriel  oriel.ox.ac.uk/study-with-us/outreach-and-schools-liaison/what-we-do/events-and-prizes/rex-nettleford-prize-for-year-12-students
